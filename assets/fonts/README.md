# Fonts

General Sans by the Indian Type Foundry, from Fontshare
(https://www.fontshare.com/fonts/general-sans), under the ITF Free Font
License, which allows free personal and commercial use, including on the web.

Served from this site rather than a font CDN (see `font-src 'self'` in the
CSP, `includes/auth.php`). Weights 400, 500, 600 and 700, woff2 only; the
faces are declared at the top of `assets/css/design.css`.
